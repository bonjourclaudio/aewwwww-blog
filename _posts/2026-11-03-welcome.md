This is the shared blog for our group. Each of us posts process notes, research and work-in-progress here, and the professors can follow along.

## Writing a post

1. Go to [app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org) and sign in with GitHub.
2. Open this blog's repository and click **Posts → Add an entry**.
3. Fill in the title, pick your name and write. The toolbar handles headings, bold, lists and links, the way a normal text editor does.
4. To add images, click the image button in the toolbar or drag a photo in.
5. Click **Save**. The post goes live about a minute later.

To keep something as a draft, untick **Published** before saving.
